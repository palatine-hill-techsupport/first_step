First Step digital prototype

A safer first step into support, study, work, and confidence.

first_step is a low-pressure bridge for young people aged 16 to 25 facing housing instability, homelessness risk, or barriers to financial stability.

Find a next step See the pilot
Not a 24/7 crisis service. This prototype is a supported pathway and referral bridge. Crisis, housing, and youth work support must stay with qualified services and partners.

Mission

Make the first step easier before everything becomes a collapse.

The hardest point is often before a young person is connected, referred, and ready enough to join a full program. first_step creates small, practical entry points that do not force disclosure or make someone prove they are struggling.

The model connects public pop-ups, a free digital platform, youth-worker support, TAFE and VCE VM options, existing social enterprises, and paid placement pathways.

Suggested first step

Start with a youth-worker check-in.

A short conversation can map what is urgent, what can wait, and which partner pathway is safest. No one needs to share a full story at a booth or in chat.

  • Confirm immediate safety and basic needs.
  • Map one support, study, or work option.
  • Offer a warm referral instead of another cold link.

Free platform

Plain-language tools for low-energy days.

Resume help

Short video scripts, simple resume prompts, and a one-page first-job template.

Interview support

Practice answers, what to wear, what to bring, and what to say if life is messy.

Practical support

Food, transport, clothing, hygiene, and local support options before a shift.

Pathway map

TAFE, VCE VM, paid placement, youth service, and social enterprise next steps.

Pilot format

A public pop-up with a quieter digital door.

The pilot combines a sponsor-funded merch stall with youth-worker-supported conversations and a mobile-first online pathway. A young person can browse, scan, talk, take information away, or come back later through chat or video.

  1. 1

    Find the stall

    At a TAFE campus, VCE VM event, council youth expo, market, or service open day.

  2. 2

    Choose pressure level

    Browse merch, scan the platform, speak privately, or leave with a simple card.

  3. 3

    Take a second step

    Warm referral, paid placement lead, study option, support check-in, or follow-up.

Partner model

first_step designs and coordinates the roads between.

Youth workers

Consent-led engagement, referral boundaries, safeguarding, and follow-up.

TAFEs and VCE VM

Study pathways, campus access, events, and practical learning options.

Social enterprises

Work-readiness models, paid placement pathways, and proven support settings.

Youth services

Specialist housing, safety, mental health, and case-management referrals.

Sponsor proposition

A visible pathway, not a logo stuck on a good cause.

Sponsors fund booth delivery, youth-worker support, platform access, pathway contributions, and impact reporting. The exchange is visibility, staff engagement, youth employment alignment, CSR value, and measurable next-step outcomes.

Pilot measures

  • Booth interactions and platform visits
  • Guided pathways completed
  • Warm referrals made and accepted
  • Paid placement opportunities created
  • Participant, partner, and sponsor feedback
