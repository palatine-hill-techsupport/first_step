# Safeguarding assumptions

This document records implementation assumptions. It is not approval to operate.

## Service boundary

- first_step is scheduled support, not an emergency or 24-hour service.
- Urgent selections immediately show external services and 000 guidance.
- Response hours remain visible in scheduled-chat and booking contexts.
- Quick exit navigates immediately to a configurable neutral URL and does not claim to erase history.

## Workforce

- Every live worker must have qualifications, role scope, screening, supervision and escalation authority reviewed by qualified governance.
- The platform stores broad outcomes only. It is not a clinical record or case-management system.
- Meeting links are private and added later by authorised staff.

## Young people aged 16–17

- The age band is collected without date of birth.
- Consent, capacity, mandatory reporting, guardian involvement and safe-contact practice require specific Victorian legal and youth-work review.
- Product copy must not promise confidentiality beyond lawful limits. Final disclosure wording is not yet approved.

## Crisis and family violence

- External details need a named verification owner and review schedule.
- Family-violence pathways, quick exit, contact safety and worker escalation need specialist review.
- Staff must have an approved protocol for disclosures of immediate danger.

## Referrals

- A worker explains the proposed partner and data before consent.
- Only approved contact fields, broad topics and a participant-approved summary are sent.
- Partner agreements, secure transmission, contact failures and consent withdrawal require operational procedures.

## Reviews required before production

Youth-work governance, privacy, safeguarding, legal, under-18 consent, records retention, crisis escalation, worker safety, referral partner assurance, security and accessibility reviews are all mandatory.

**A technically working booking system does not itself make first_step ready to deliver youth-work services.**
